The Engineering Philosophy

Throughout the history of sports science, athletes have been forced to accept a broken compromise.

You wear a sleek smart ring to bed to record resting heart rate, circadian rhythms, and nocturnal recovery. Internal recovery was completely isolated from external load.

Why standard wearables leave athletes stranded

Most consumer fitness trackers are made for casual 10,000-step walkers, not high-intensity athletic competition.

The Optical PPG Latency

Optical sensors on the wrist or finger rely on blood capillary refilling. When you sprint from 0 to 32 km/h in 3 seconds, optical heart rate lags by 8 to 15 seconds. Apex Vest's 250Hz dry ECG detects cardiac electrical impulse instantly.

Contact Sports Rules

Referees in FIFA football, rugby, basketball, and combat sports ban watches and hard metal rings due to laceration and finger injury risks. The soft compression vest is fully FIFA and World Rugby approved for match play.

4. Step-by-Step Instructions (How to Use) +
  1. Download App: Install the Ultrahuman app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Create your athlete account.
  2. Initial Charge: Place Ring Nova on its wireless dock. Plug the Apex Pod into its magnetic cable. Allow 60 minutes for 100% charge.
  3. Bluetooth Pairing: Open the app, tap Add Device, select Dual Suite, and hold both devices within 10 cm of your smartphone.
  4. Wearing Ring Nova: Slide onto the index, middle, or ring finger of your non-dominant hand. Ensure sensor bumps touch the palm side. Wear 24/7 (including sleep).
  5. Wearing Apex Vest: Put on the vest directly against your skin under your jersey or training top. Ensure the dry chest electrodes sit flat below the pectorals. Lightly dampen the electrodes with water if skin is dry before cold-weather training.
  6. Inserting Pod: Snap the Apex Pod into the pouch between your shoulder blades with the logo facing outward. Tap the pod twice to start Match Tracking (LED flashes green).
  7. Post-Match Sync: Upon completion, tap the pod twice to stop. Open the app to instantly download your high-speed match telemetry and view combined recovery insights.
5. Safety Precautions & Warnings +
WARNING: This device is designed for sports, fitness, and wellness optimization. It is NOT a diagnostic medical ECG device. Do not use for diagnosing heart murmurs or acute cardiac events.
  • Do not wear the metal Ring Nova while operating high-torque heavy industrial machinery or during heavy barbell lifts without grip chalk.
  • Remove the Apex Sensor Pod before washing the compression garment. Washing the pod in a washing machine will void the warranty.
  • If you experience redness or skin irritation under the chest electrodes, discontinue vest use and wash skin with mild soap.
  • Keep charging docks and USB cables away from water baths and open rain.
6. Maintenance & Washing Instructions +

Apex Vest Garment: Always remove the sensor pod first. Machine wash cold (30°C / 85°F) on gentle cycle with mild detergent. Do NOT use fabric softeners or chlorine bleach. Air dry in shade — do NOT tumble dry or iron.

Ring Nova: Clean once per week under warm running water using mild, fragrance-free soap. Dry thoroughly with a microfiber cloth. Ensure the interior resin sensor windows remain free from soap buildup.

Sensor Pod: Wipe clean with an alcohol-free sanitizing wipe. Do not submerge pod in water.
